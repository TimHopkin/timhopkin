The Crisis We're Facing Let's Talk

We're Witnessing the Collapse in Real-Time

The recent wave of European farming protests isn't about policy disagreement. It's about survival.

Land managers—farmers stewarding Britain's 17 million hectares—face impossible administrative burdens:

  • Navigate 30+ different environmental schemes, each with different rules
  • Provide the same data to multiple agencies in different formats
  • Wait months for payment decisions with zero visibility into processing
  • Make £100,000+ land management decisions with no reliable data

Meanwhile, investors have billions ready to deploy into nature restoration. They can't find trustworthy projects. Farmers need capital desperately. They can't access it.

The two sides can't find each other because there's no infrastructure connecting them.

My grandfather's farm—four generations of careful stewardship—is at risk of being sold. So are 100,000 others across Britain.

Not because farmers are failing. Because the system is broken.

Why I'm Building This

I'm the fourth generation on Norney Farm—90 acres south of Guildford where my grandfather ran a Jersey herd demonstration farm. His ashes are scattered on that land.

He taught me three principles:

  1. The principle of leverage - achieve maximum return with minimum effort
  2. "Think about it" - deeply understand how systems work
  3. "Rub it on" - persevere through difficulty

That farm is now at risk. Not because of bad farming—because there's no viable economic path forward for land stewardship in Britain's current system.

In 2015, I joined Geovation (Ordnance Survey's innovation program) with a question: What if we could connect the entire land economy value chain? What if farmers could prove their impact to retailers and government in real-time? What if investment capital could flow to land restoration as easily as it flows to tech startups?

A decade later, we're making it real:

  • Digital Land Solutions: 22-person team building the digital infrastructure
  • Waitrose partnership: Onboarding their entire supply chain to prove the model works
  • UK Government: Building the Land Use Framework to enable blended public/private finance
  • Regenerative Surrey: Demonstrating the approach in my home county

This isn't a consultancy. It's infrastructure. The kind of foundational building block that makes markets possible.

If we get this right, my grandfather's farm—and 100,000 others—has a path forward. If we don't, we lose them.
