UK National Risk Register: Building Resilience from the Ground Up

How strategic land management is the key to addressing the UK's most pressing national security risks

"National resilience is not just about emergency response; it is about the fundamental robustness of our critical infrastructure, of which our land is the most vital component."

Understanding the Risks

The UK National Risk Register identifies a range of threats, from pandemic disease to severe weather and supply chain disruption. A common thread running through many of these risks is our reliance on a functioning, productive landscape.

Food security, water security, and flood defense are all directly dependent on how we manage our land. Neglecting our natural capital leaves us exposed and vulnerable to shocks.

Strategic Land Use

To build true national resilience, we must adopt a strategic approach to land use. This means moving beyond a fragmented, parcel-by-parcel view to a coordinated national strategy. We need to identify where our land can best deliver food, where it can best store water, and where it can best sequester carbon.

Digital tools allow us to model these scenarios and optimize our land use for resilience. We can design landscapes that buffer against extreme weather while maintaining productivity.

A Unified Approach

This requires a unified approach across government departments. DEFRA, the Cabinet Office, and the Treasury must work together to value and invest in our land as critical national infrastructure. By aligning policy and funding, we can secure the UK's future against the growing threats of the 21st century.
