The Norney Farm Blueprint: Exemplifying the 8 Forms of Capital

A living laboratory for regenerative land management and sustainable economics

"Norney Farm is more than just a farm; it is a proof of concept for how we can regenerate our land while building multiple forms of capital—financial, social, and ecological."

A Living Laboratory

At Norney Farm, we are putting theory into practice. By applying the principles of regenerative agriculture and permaculture, we are demonstrating that it is possible to restore soil health, increase biodiversity, and produce high-quality food, all while running a viable business.

The 8 Forms of Capital

Our approach is guided by the framework of the 8 Forms of Capital. We are not just focused on financial return, but on growing our:

  • Living Capital: Soil health, biodiversity, and ecosystem services.
  • Social Capital: Community engagement and networks.
  • Intellectual Capital: Knowledge sharing and educational programs.
  • Experiential Capital: "Learning by doing" and practical skills.

The Blueprint for the Future

We believe that the model we are developing at Norney Farm can serve as a blueprint for others. By sharing our successes and our challenges, we hope to inspire and empower other land managers to embark on their own regenerative journeys.
