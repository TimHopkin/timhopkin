## TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### Design Principles
1. **White space**: Don't cram. Each section breathes
2. **Visual hierarchy**: Use size/weight to guide eye
3. **One idea per section**: Don't mix messages
4. **Mobile-first**: 60%+ traffic will be mobile
5. **Fast loading**: Compress images, minimal animations

### Color Psychology
- **Primary color**: Deep green or earth tone (trust, nature, stability)
- **Accent color**: Warm orange/gold (energy, optimism, action)
- **Background**: Clean white or very light gray
- **Text**: Dark gray, not pure black (easier on eyes)

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Bold, modern sans-serif (Helvetica, Inter, Proxima Nova)
- **Body**: Readable serif (Georgia, Lora) or clean sans-serif
- **Size**: 18-20px body text minimum (readability)

### Images Needed
1. Professional headshot of you (approx. square format)
2. Norney Farm aerial or landscape shot
3. Systems diagram (farmers→retailers→government flow)
4. Before/after diagram (fragmented vs connected system)
5. Screenshot of LandApp or work product
6. Map visual (Regenerative Surrey or UK coverage)
7. Optional: Farmers in field, British countryside

### Key Metrics to Track
- Time on page (should increase dramatically)
- Scroll depth (are people reading to bottom?)
- Click-through on free resource (reciprocity working?)
- Contact form submissions / calendar bookings
- Bounce rate (should decrease)
